Privacy

Morning Flight is made by Box Nutrition & Performance Ltd (trading as Metabolic Birmingham), Birmingham, UK. Last updated 8 October 2026.

What the app stores

Your mornings, how you said you felt, your one thing for the day and whether you did it, your weekly reflections, your goal and your settings. By default all of this stays on your device and is never sent to us.

The pre-flight check

The check asks the five WHO-5 wellbeing questions and five body-clock questions. Your answers and scores stay on your device. They are only sent to us if you choose "Save my results" and tick the consent box. Then we store your email, your wellbeing score, your body-clock type and your suggested flight time, so we can send you your two-week re-check and tell you when the iPhone app is available. Wellbeing scores are health-related information, so we only store them with your explicit consent, in our database hosted by Supabase in Ireland. You can withdraw consent and have them deleted at any time by emailing us.

If you sign in with your email

Signing in is optional. If you do, your email address and the data above are stored in our account database (hosted by Supabase in Ireland) so your mornings follow you to another phone. We use your email only to send sign-in emails and to keep your account working. We do not sell it or share it with advertisers.

Subscriptions

Morning Flight Premium is bought through Apple. Purchases are processed by Apple and recorded by RevenueCat, which we use to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID (or your account ID if you're signed in) and your purchase history, not your payment details. Cancel any time in your iPhone Settings under your Apple ID, Subscriptions.

Notifications

If you turn on a morning notification, the phone schedules it locally. On the website, notifications are delivered by OneSignal, which stores an anonymous device token and your chosen time.

Sharing

Share cards show the word of the day and your morning count. Your intentions and reflections are never on a share card.

Analytics and ads

None. No tracking, no advertising, no third-party analytics.

Deleting your data

In the app: Your log, "Delete all my data from this device" erases everything stored on your phone. If you've signed in, Your account, "Delete my account and data" permanently deletes your account and everything we store for it. Signed out, delete the app (or clear the website's data) and everything is gone. For an account, email hello@themorningflight.co.uk from the address you signed in with and we will delete it within 7 days.
